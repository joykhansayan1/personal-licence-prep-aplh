Restore purchases

Open the app → Settings → Restore Purchases. You can also restore from the paywall screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used to buy.

Manage or cancel a subscription

On your iPhone or iPad: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Personal Licence Prep APLH. Or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
