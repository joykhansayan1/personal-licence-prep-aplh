Support

Personal Licence Prep APLH

How can we help?

Email dmjks120@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as possible. Include your device model (e.g. iPhone 15 or Pixel 8) and iOS or Android version if you are reporting a bug.

Restore purchases

Open the app → Settings → Restore Purchases. You can also restore from the paywall screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID (or, on Android, Google account) used to buy.

Manage or cancel a subscription

On your iPhone or iPad: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Personal Licence Prep APLH. Or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

On Android: open the Google Play app → tap your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Personal Licence Prep APLH → Cancel subscription. Cancel before the 3-day free trial ends to avoid being charged.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, so we cannot issue refunds directly. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone, iPad) or through Google Play (Android).
