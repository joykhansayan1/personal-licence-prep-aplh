Privacy Policy

Personal Licence Prep APLH · Last updated 16 July 2026

Summary

Personal Licence Prep APLH does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. The app works entirely offline. Your study progress, answers, bookmarks and settings are stored only on your device.

Information we collect

None. We do not require an account, a name, an email address, or a login. We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

To make the app work, the following is saved locally on your device only (never transmitted to us):

  • Your study progress, quiz results, bookmarks and streaks.
  • Your preferences (daily goal, optional exam date, reminder time).

This data is removed if you delete the app or reset progress in Settings.

Notifications

If you enable the optional daily study reminder, notifications are scheduled locally on your device by iOS or Android. No notification data leaves your device. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app, in iOS Settings, or in Android Settings → Apps → Personal Licence Prep APLH → Notifications.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit (iPhone, iPad) or by Google through Google Play Billing (Android). We never see or store your payment information or your Apple Account or Google account details. Purchase validation is handled on your device by Apple or Google Play. Payment data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy or Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for a personal alcohol licence and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
